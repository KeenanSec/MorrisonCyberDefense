### B. Startup Capitalization & Working Capital

* **Zero Debt Policy:** The business will launch with zero commercial bank debt or outside investor equity.
* **Initial Capitalization:** $5,000 self-funded cash reserve deposited into an Amegy Bank or Frost Bank Texas commercial checking account.
* **Operating Reserve:** Maintain a 3-month trailing expense reserve in a High-Yield Business Savings account before taking quarterly partner distributions.
* **Working Capital Architecture:** 100% automated credit card or ACH debit on the **1st of each month** in advance for retainers; 50% deposit upfront on projects. Zero Net-30 accounts receivable risk.

---

### C. Contingency & Family Business Succession Plan

1. **WGU Competency Prioritization:** Because WGU is competency-based and self-paced, degree milestones and client fieldwork reinforce each other without scheduling deadlocks.
2. **Key-Person Incapacity:** If the Principal Architect is temporarily incapacitated, all active implementation sprints pause; retainer clients receive an automatic monthly fee credit, and technical monitoring continues under senior external advisory network backstops.
3. **Graduate Transition Plan (2027):** Upon Keenan's graduation from WGU with his B.S. in Cybersecurity & Information Assurance, equity adjusts to **50/50 equal partnership**, and the firm initiates hiring its first non-family Junior Security Technician.
