### 1.1 The 24/7 SOC / MDR Contradiction: A Fatal Legal Exposure

* **The Vulnerability:** Marketing materials that claim *"24/7 Managed EDR"* or *"24/7 Threat Detection"* while the internal team has no one awake at 3:00 AM create massive legal liability. If a client is encrypted by ransomware on a Sunday morning, their attorney will sue for breach of contract, and your E&O insurance carrier will deny coverage under deceptive trade practice exclusions.
* **The Solution:** Sublicense **Huntress Labs Managed EDR + Managed ITDR (M365)** through distributor **Pax8** (zero minimum commitment, ~$5/user/mo). 
  * Huntress provides a real, human-staffed 24/7 SOC that isolates compromised endpoints and revokes hijacked M365 sessions at 3:00 AM without waking you up.
  * You legally and accurately market: *"Managed Cloud Defense backed by 24/7 SOC Threat Isolation."*

---

### 1.2 The $1,500 Fixed-Price Scope Creep Trap

* **The Problem:** A flat $1,500 assessment without seat limits or diagnostic boundaries inevitably turns into 40+ hours of unpaid IT troubleshooting when you discover rogue domain controllers, personal devices, and unmanaged switches.
* **The Solution:** Implement the **Diagnostic Scope Box (Schedule A)**:
  1. Exactly 1 production M365 tenant and up to 2 domain perimeters.
  2. Representative sampling capped at 5 physical endpoints.
  3. **Zero Active Remediation:** Diagnostics only. No password resets, line-of-business software troubleshooting, or infrastructure re-engineering under the diagnostic SOW.

---

### 1.3 Working Capital & Cash Flow (Death by Net-30)

* **The Trap:** If you invoice Houston commercial contractors on Net-30 terms, they will pay on **Net-60 or Net-90** due to construction industry "pay-when-paid" cycles.
* **The Solution:**
  * **One-Off Projects:** 50% deposit upon contract execution; 50% due on deliverable presentation (reports delivered upon receipt of funds).
  * **Recurring Retainers:** 100% automated credit card or ACH debit on the **1st of every month** in advance via Stripe or QuickBooks Payments. Zero Net-30 invoices.
