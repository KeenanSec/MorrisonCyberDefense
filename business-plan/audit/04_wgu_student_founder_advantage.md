### 4.1 The WGU Competency-Based Academic Advantage

Initial startup playbooks assumed the student founder was trapped in a rigid, traditional university schedule with compulsory 8:00 AM lectures and unyielding midterm exam weeks.

**The Reality of Western Governors University (WGU):**
* **100% Asynchronous & Competency-Based:** WGU courses do not require sitting in physical classrooms. Progression is measured by passing objective assessments and hands-on performance assessments.
* **Full Daytime Flexibility:** Keenan can conduct client discovery calls, coordinate with IT managers, and run tenant audits during standard Houston business hours (`8:00 AM – 5:00 PM CT`).
* **Work-to-Degree Acceleration:** Real-world consulting fieldwork (deploying Conditional Access, auditing Microsoft Graph APIs, configuring DMARC) directly maps to WGU's Cybersecurity & Information Assurance curriculum. Practical consulting accelerates academic completion.

### 4.2 Why Keenan MUST Be the Co-Founder

A common error in family businesses is introducing the younger partner as *"my son who is in college helping out"* or an *"associate analyst."*

#### Why "Co-Founder" Commands Instant Client Respect:
1. **Legal Adult Status:** Born October 29, 2007 (18 years old, turning 19), Keenan is a full legal adult empowered under Texas LLC law to be a Managing Member and Co-Founder.
2. **Skin in the Game:** Houston business owners love doing business with authentic family operators who have personal pride on the line. When clients realize Keenan is a **Co-Founder**, they recognize he isn't an hourly employee who might quit; he is personally invested in protecting their business.
3. **Triple-Certified Technical Execution:** Holding CompTIA Security+, Network+, and A+ at age 18 demonstrates elite focus. Backed by 20+ years of enterprise governance from the Senior Partner, this two-tier leadership model is virtually impossible for generalist IT competitors to match.

#### The Client Introduction Script:
> *"At Morrison Cyber Defense, our practice is anchored by two disciplines: Enterprise Architectural Governance and Hands-On Systems Auditing. I lead our architectural review, governance alignment, and executive risk strategy.*  
> 
> *My Co-Founder, Keenan Morrison, leads our technical execution and systems auditing. He holds three industry credentials including CompTIA Security+, is recognized nationally in hands-on competition, and accelerates his cybersecurity degree through WGU. Keenan personally oversees the tenant extraction scripts and evidence verification under my architectural parameters."*

To maintain complete corporate boundaries:
