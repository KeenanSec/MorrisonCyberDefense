### Core Governance Principles
1. **Equal 50/50 Ownership:** Keenan Morrison and Senior Partner hold equal voting units and economic interests.
2. **Strict Non-Commingling:** All operations flow through dedicated commercial banking (Amegy/Frost Bank Texas). Zero personal commingling.
3. **Founder Scope Separation:** Keenan Morrison leads technical systems auditing, tenant discovery, CIPP automation, and patch validation; Senior Partner leads executive enterprise architecture, client briefings, and broker relationships.
4. **Succession & Incapacity Safety:** Built-in protocol ensuring uninterrupted client protection under Huntress 24/7 MDR backstop during temporary incapacity, with structured estate buy-sell options.
