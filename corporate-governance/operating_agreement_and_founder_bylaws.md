### 1. Company Formation & Governance

1.1 **Formation:** Morrison Cyber Defense LLC (the "Company") has been organized as a Texas limited liability company pursuant to the Texas Business Organizations Code (TBOC) by filing Certificate of Formation with the Secretary of State of Texas.

1.2 **Principal Place of Business:** The principal executive office of the Company is located in the Greater Houston Area, Harris County, Texas, or at such other location within the State of Texas as the Members may unanimously determine.

1.3 **Purpose:** The primary purpose of the Company is to provide commercial cybersecurity advisory services, Microsoft 365 defense hardening, continuous threat detection and managed security response (MDR), cybersecurity insurance readiness auditing, and related technological and security consulting to commercial enterprises, and to engage in any lawful business permitted under Texas law.

1.4 **Tax Status:** The Company shall initially be classified and taxed as a Partnership (or an S-Corporation upon formal IRS Form 2553 election approved unanimously by the Members).

1.5 **Term:** The Company shall have perpetual existence unless dissolved in accordance with this Agreement or the TBOC.

### 3. Allocations of Profits, Losses & Distributions

3.1 **Allocation of Net Profits and Losses:** Net profits and net losses of the Company for each fiscal year shall be allocated to each Member strictly in proportion to their respective percentage ownership interest (50% / 50%).

3.2 **Operating Cash Reserve Target:** The Company shall maintain a rolling minimum operating cash reserve equal to three (3) months of fixed operational expenses (minimum $5,000.00) in its primary checking account.

3.3 **Distributions:** Distributable cash shall be calculated after satisfying all operational liabilities, SaaS tool licenses (Sherweb, Huntress, CIPP, PSA), insurance amortizations, and the required cash reserve. Distributions shall be made monthly or quarterly as unanimously determined by the Members, divided 50/50.

### 4. Management, Voting & Decision Rights

4.1 **Member-Managed Structure:** The Company is member-managed. Except as otherwise provided herein, all ordinary business decisions require the mutual consensus of both Members.

4.2 **Major Business Decisions (Unanimous Consent Required):** The following actions require the prior written unanimous affirmative consent of both Members:
* Incurring any commercial debt, loan, or credit line exceeding $1,000.00.
* Entering into commercial real estate leases or capital equipment purchases exceeding $2,500.00.
* Admitting any new Member, issuing equity, or altering ownership percentages.
* Hiring full-time or W-2 employees.
* Approving annual operating budgets and tax filings (IRS Form 1065 / 1120-S).
* Engaging outside legal counsel or CPA firms.
* Merging, acquiring, reorganizing, or dissolving the Company.

4.3 **Day-to-Day Operational Authority:** Each Member is granted individual authority to execute vendor purchase orders, client proposals, non-disclosure agreements, and standard Statements of Work under $10,000.00 within their designated operational scope defined in Section 5.

### 6. Transfers of Membership Interests & Buy-Sell Provisions

6.1 **Transfer Restriction:** No Member may sell, assign, pledge, encumber, or otherwise transfer all or any part of their membership interest without the prior written unanimous consent of the other Member.

6.2 **Right of First Refusal (ROFR):** In the event a Member receives a bona fide third-party offer to purchase their interest, the non-selling Member shall have an absolute 60-day Right of First Refusal to purchase said interest under identical financial terms.

6.3 **Drag-Along & Tag-Along Rights:** Standard drag-along and tag-along protections apply to any bona fide institutional purchase offer for 100% of Company equity.

### 7. Incapacity, Dissolution & Succession Architecture

7.1 **Key-Person Temporary Incapacity:** If either Member suffers a temporary medical disability or incapacity:
* Active project sprints (Tier 2) shall immediately pause, with client milestone dates adjusted accordingly.
* Recurring Retainer clients (Tier 3) will continue under automated 24/7 Huntress SOC endpoint isolation and Microsoft 365 cloud monitoring.
* The healthy Member shall assume temporary single-manager operational authority for recurring service stewardship.

7.2 **Permanent Incapacity or Death (Succession Plan):**
* The deceased or incapacitated Member’s estate or designated heir shall be entitled only to economic rights (distributions of profits) and shall possess no voting, managerial, or operational rights.
* The surviving Member shall have an exclusive option, exercisable within 180 days, to purchase the economic interest of the deceased Member at fair market valuation (determined by an independent certified business valuator) funded by Company key-person life insurance proceeds or a 36-month promissory note at prime interest rate.

7.3 **WGU Graduate Transition Architecture (2027 Milestone):**
* Upon Keenan Morrison’s completion of his B.S. in Cybersecurity from WGU, both Members shall conduct a formal strategic governance review to evaluate corporate conversion, first junior technician hiring, and commercial real estate acquisition.

### 8. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property Assignment

8.1 **Assignment of Inventions:** All scripts, audit baselines, CIPP automation policies, reporting templates, proprietary questionnaires, and technical methodologies created by either Member in connection with Company business are the exclusive property of Morrison Cyber Defense LLC.

8.2 **Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation:** During membership and for a period of twelve (12) months following withdrawal from the Company, neither Member shall solicit Company clients, divert referral broker relationships, or operate a competing managed cybersecurity consultancy within the State of Texas without express written consent.

### 9. General Provisions & Texas Law Mandate

9.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be construed, interpreted, and enforced strictly in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without giving effect to conflict of law principles.

9.2 **Venue & Dispute Resolution:** Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved through mandatory non-binding mediation in Houston, Harris County, Texas. If mediation fails, the matter shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Houston, Texas under the commercial rules of the American Arbitration Association (AAA).

9.3 **Entire Agreement:** This Operating Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Members concerning the governance and operation of the Company, superseding all prior oral or written discussions.

9.4 **Amendments:** This Agreement may be amended or modified only by a written instrument signed by both Members.
