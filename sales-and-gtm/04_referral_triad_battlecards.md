### Partner Battlecard 1: Commercial Cyber Insurance Brokers
* **Target Firms in Houston:** McGriff, USI, Higginbotham, Insurica, Bowen Miclette & Britt.
* **Their Problem:** Insurance underwriters (Chubb, Travelers, Coalition) are issuing conditional non-renewals or demanding 200%+ rate hikes on commercial contractors lacking MFA and EDR. Brokers risk losing commissions.
* **The Pitch Script:**  
  > *"We are Morrison Cyber Defense. We don't sell general IT, printers, or desktop hardware. We are pure-play security engineers. When your commercial accounts get hit with an impossible 5-page cyber questionnaire or conditional non-renewal, send them to us. We will conduct a rapid 72-hour Insurance Readiness Sprint, deploy the exact controls required, and give you a certified attestation letter so you can bind the policy on time."*

---

### Partner Battlecard 2: Non-Security Local IT MSPs
* **Target Firms in Houston:** Over 400 small IT firms (1–5 technicians) across Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery counties.
* **Their Problem:** They configure routers and reset passwords, but fear getting sued when a client gets hit with ransomware. They lack full-time cybersecurity architects.
* **The Pitch Script (The Non-Compete Covenant):**  
  > *"We deliver co-managed cybersecurity sprints under a strict, attorney-reviewed Non-Compete Covenant. We do not provide desktop support, printer maintenance, or hardware resale. You keep 100% of your IT relationship; we provide the enterprise cybersecurity credentials that protect your client and protect you from liability."*

---

### Partner Battlecard 3: Regional Bank Commercial Loan Officers
* **Target Banks in Houston:** Amegy Bank, Frost Bank, Texas Capital, Cadence Bank.
* **Their Problem:** Borrowers in construction and energy wiring $500k draws to fraudulent accounts due to business email compromise (BEC).
* **The Pitch Script:**  
  > *"We offer your commercial borrowers a complimentary 15-minute Wire Transfer & Dual-Custody Email Security Review to verify their payment systems cannot be redirected by email spoofing."*
