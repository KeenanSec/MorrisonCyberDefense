### Strategic Context: Why Insurance Brokers Are Our #1 Customer Acquisition Channel

In the commercial middle market ($5M–$50M revenue), business owners rarely buy cybersecurity because they want to—they buy it because **they are forced to**. The single most powerful enforcement mechanism today is the **Commercial Cyber Insurance Renewal**.

Underwriters (e.g., Travelers, Beazley, Chubb, Coalition, CFC) have instituted strict minimum technical requirements:
1. **Phishing-resistant Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** across all administrative and user accounts without legacy protocol bypasses.
2. **Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)** with active behavioral blocking and 24/7 isolation capabilities.
3. **Immutable or Air-Gapped Backups** protected from domain administrative compromise.
4. **Email Security Protections (SPF, DKIM, and strict DMARC policies)** to block business email compromise (BEC).

When an insured commercial contractor or supplier fails an underwriter questionnaire, the commercial insurance broker faces a crisis:
* The underwriter issues a **conditional non-renewal notice** or a **200%–300% premium hike**.
* The policy cannot bind by the expiration date.
* The broker risks losing the account, commissions, and client goodwill.

**Morrison Cyber Defense solves this exact problem for the broker.** We act as the rapid-response remediation team that fixes the technical gaps, satisfies the underwriter's requirements, and provides technical documentation so the broker can bind the policy on time.

## Part 3: Broker Relationship Management & Operational Rules

1. **Never Disparage the Client's Existing IT Provider:**
   Brokers are protective of client relationships. If an insured's existing IT setup is sloppy, our framing is always constructive: *"We partner with the existing IT team to take the burden of advanced security configurations off their plate so you can satisfy the underwriter."*
2. **Never Guarantee Underwriter Acceptance:**
   We never say: *"We guarantee your client will pass and get coverage."*  
   We say: *"We deploy and objectively verify the technical controls specified by the carrier's questionnaire, providing complete implementation evidence for underwriter review."*
3. **No Unlicensed Referral Fees (Texas Insurance Code Compliance):**
   In Texas, paying or receiving rebates or unlicensed commissions on insurance transactions is illegal. We do NOT offer or accept referral commissions on insurance premiums. Our value proposition to the broker is operational: we save their accounts, protect their commission from being lost to a competitor, and eliminate non-renewal stress.
4. **Log All Broker Interactions in the Company Harness:**
   Track every producer outreach, conversation, and client referral using the local harness:
