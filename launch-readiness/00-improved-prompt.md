# Improved prompt

Audit the existing MorrisonCyberDefense repository as a practical launch adviser for a two-person cybersecurity consultancy. Determine what is needed to start prospect conversations, sign the first appropriately scoped client, and deliver that engagement reliably. Distinguish verified assets, missing artifacts, and real-world facts that cannot be verified from files. Do not treat plans, projections, sample findings, or vendor names as proof of operational readiness.

Prioritize the smallest useful setup. Identify misleading sales claims and scope contradictions. Recommend one initial customer profile and one deliverable service, clearly labeling proposals that require founder decisions. Verify relevant Texas and federal outreach/business requirements using primary sources; distinguish legal obligations from recommended business practices and unresolved applicability questions.

Write an actionable readiness assessment, a checklist with suggested owners and completion evidence, a narrow offer sheet, honest phone/email scripts, discovery questions, a proposal outline, and a first-ten-business-days plan. List what can wait. Do not send messages, register businesses, purchase services, invent credentials or testimonials, or claim any real-world prerequisite is complete. Link the results from the project README.
