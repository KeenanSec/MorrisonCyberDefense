# Outreach and business checks

Checked against the sources below on September 23, 2026. These are targeted preparation notes, not a determination that every law applies to your particular campaign or service. Obtain qualified advice for unresolved applicability; the repository does not establish your entity, insurance, tax, or registration status.

## Before calling

Texas telephone-solicitation registration depends on the activity and available exemptions. The Secretary of State places the applicability determination on the seller. Record the basis for registration or exemption before launching the campaign; do not assume “B2B” settles Texas requirements. [Texas SOS telephone solicitation FAQ](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/statdoc/faqs3400.shtml).

Federal B2B calls are not a blanket exemption from truthful-selling requirements: the FTC expanded misrepresentation prohibitions to B2B telemarketing in 2024. Avoid invented customers, false urgency, and unsupported coverage or compliance outcomes. [FTC rule announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/03/ftc-implements-new-protections-businesses-against-telemarketing-fraud-affirms-protections-against-ai).

Recommended initial operating scope: manually placed calls to verified published business lines during local business hours, accurate caller identity, a suppression list, and no automated/prerecorded calls, unsolicited texts, or recording. This is a simplification, not a legal exemption. Confirm applicable federal/state calling, do-not-call, and recordkeeping rules for the actual numbers, locations, and technology. If adding recording, texting, personal mobile numbers, or automation, review those requirements before use.

## Before marketing email

CAN-SPAM covers commercial B2B email. Use accurate headers and subjects, disclose the commercial nature appropriately, include a valid physical postal address, offer a clear opt-out, and honor opt-outs within ten business days. Keep the opt-out mechanism working for at least 30 days after sending. Hiring another sender does not remove responsibility. [FTC compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

Operational recommendation: suppress opt-outs immediately and keep proof of requested follow-ups. Do not treat a publicly listed email as evidence of consent for other channels.

## Before signing and billing

Verify legal identity and authority using actual company records; the Texas SOS provides entity search and filing-verification routes. An LLC suffix in a README is not verification. Also confirm ownership decisions and the right to use the business name. [Texas SOS searches](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/searches.shtml).

Confirm EIN/tax identity as applicable, a business payment route, invoice details, bookkeeping ownership, and customer vendor-onboarding documents such as a W-9 when requested. These are verification tasks; no sensitive identifiers belong in the public repository.

Do not assume every cybersecurity service is taxable or exempt in Texas. The Comptroller distinguishes professional consulting from taxable data processing and separately discusses taxable security services. Have the actual assessment, managed-service, software-resale, and bundled invoice items classified before billing; confirm any required permit and collection procedure. [Texas data processing guidance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/94-127.php), [Texas security-services guidance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/94-119.php).

Licensing applicability also depends on actual work. Do not infer that calling the business “cyber defense” establishes a private-security license requirement. If expanding into investigations, physical security, or other regulated services, check the exact scope with qualified advisers and the relevant agency. The DPS compilation expressly distinguishes certain cybersecurity-event analysis from investigations. [Texas DPS statutes and rules](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/rsd/psb/docs/psStatutesAndRules2024.pdf). Recheck current law for any expansion; this older compilation is not a current licensing clearance.

## Before work begins

Recommended company policy: confirm appropriate technology E&O/cyber coverage and any contract-required insurance before client work. This audit does not establish a universal statutory insurance minimum. Ask a broker to match the actual scope, exclusions, subcontractors, incident handling, and limits; keep a coverage reference.

Have reviewed agreements, exact client authorization, appropriate access controls, an agreed evidence-handling arrangement, and a named delivery reviewer. Legal/tax review, insurance binding, account setup, and client signatures are real-world actions still to complete or verify; writing this kit does not complete them.
