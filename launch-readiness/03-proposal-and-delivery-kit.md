## Quote economics worksheet

Estimate preparation, collection, analysis, Principal review, client meeting, project administration, and rework hours separately. Multiply each by an agreed internal cost rate; add direct tool/provider costs and contingency. Compare expected contribution against the quoted fee, excluding taxes collected. Validate estimates during the rehearsal. Do not discount by automatically giving away a full assessment unless the resulting economics are acceptable.

## Proof before the first delivery

1. Use a lab or environment for which you have permission. Define the exact read-only assessment scope.
2. Write the collection runbook, required permissions, tool versions, and expected outputs. Check authoritative vendor instructions when implementing it.
3. Collect evidence and record source, date, coverage, and limitations. Store it in the intended restricted location.
4. Produce a real sample using harness/templates/report.md, evidence.csv, and findings.csv. Clearly label every page as a lab demonstration, never a customer case study.
5. Have the Principal review factual accuracy, business implications, and recommendations; resolve review comments.
6. Time the entire process. Confirm the price and delivery window can support this workload.
7. Rehearse kickoff, secure access grant, findings meeting, access revocation, and restoring a backup of working records.
8. Save the review and rehearsal results as evidence of readiness. A blank template does not pass this step.

## Minimum credibility packet

Verified founder bios with names, relevant experience and credentials; the approved offer sheet; the labeled demonstration report; tested business contact details; and an accurate account of service boundaries. Add insurance evidence when actually available and relevant. No invented logos, partnerships, clients, certifications, or testimonials.

## Contract-to-kickoff checklist

- Correct parties and signer authority confirmed.
- Final scope, price, capacity, and reviewed terms accepted.
- Signed agreement and exact authorization stored.
- Required payment milestone actually satisfied.
- Insurance and client data requirements resolved.
- Access, storage, client IT contact, emergency contact, and review date confirmed.
- Assigned implementer and Principal have reserved delivery time.

The lightweight harness already supplies the engagement records. Do not build another tracking system to perform these steps.
