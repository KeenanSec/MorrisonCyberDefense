# Getting ready to sell and deliver

Repository review: September 23, 2026. This is a practical launch assessment, not a legal opinion or verification of the actual business's formation or accounts.

**You have enough planning material. The gaps are verified contact information, a focused offer, trustworthy sales materials, confirmed commercial setup, and proof that you can deliver what you sell.** More internal workflows will not resolve these.

Suggested owners are recommendations, not accepted assignments. All unverified conditions remain open until you or your dad provide actual evidence. Store private evidence in your restricted working storage; put references rather than secrets in the checklist.

## Three separate milestones

- **Call-ready:** honest identity and offer, appropriate outreach checks, working contact channel, verified business contacts, and a next-step scheduling process.
- **Deal-ready:** confirmed contracting identity and authority, agreed scope/price/terms, reviewed agreement, payment and tax handling, insurance decision, and actual delivery capacity.
- **Delivery-ready:** signed authorization, approved access and storage, a rehearsed assessment, named reviewer, escalation plan, and a scheduled handoff.

A website redesign, recurring monitoring platform, and nine active workflows are not prerequisites for an introductory conversation. Company policies in this kit are operational recommendations unless expressly identified as sourced requirements.
