### 1. Purpose of Disclosure

The Parties wish to explore and engage in commercial discussions and potential cybersecurity service engagements, including security posture audits, Microsoft 365 cloud tenant defense, and managed detection services (the "Authorized Purpose"). In connection with the Authorized Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other Party certain proprietary, sensitive, and confidential information.

---

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information

2.1 **Scope:** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in any form or medium (written, electronic, oral, visual, or physical), disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other Party ("Receiving Party"), whether or not marked as "Confidential" or "Proprietary", that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure.

2.2 **Client Confidential Information Specifically Includes:**
* Network diagrams, topology maps, public/private IP addresses, and firewall configurations.
* Active Directory / Entra ID tenant structures, user directories, and credential security postures.
* Specific vulnerability audit findings, penetration test results, and compliance deficiency matrices.
* Internal operational workflows, proprietary client business data, and financial records.

2.3 **MCD Confidential Information Specifically Includes:**
* Proprietary assessment methodology, diagnostic checklists, and CISA ScubaGear parsing runbooks.
* Custom PowerShell automation scripts and CIPP.app tenant configuration baselines.
* Commercial pricing architecture, referral triad partner frameworks, and margin structures.
* Internal technical documentation, standard operating procedures, and training collateral.

---

### 3. Exclusions from Confidentiality

Confidential Information does not include any information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by documented evidence:
* (a) Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
* (b) Was already in the lawful possession of the Receiving Party without confidentiality restrictions prior to disclosure;
* (c) Is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without an obligation of confidentiality; or
* (d) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

---

### 4. Obligations of Receiving Party & Standard of Care

4.1 **Duty to Protect:** The Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall exercise at least the same degree of care it exercises to protect its own confidential information of like importance, but in no event less than a reasonable standard of care under the Texas Uniform Trade Secrets Act (TUTSA).

4.2 **Limited Use:** The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Authorized Purpose and for no other commercial or personal purpose.

4.3 **Restricted Disclosures:** The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party, except to its co-founders, officers, employees, legal advisors, certified public accountants, and authorized technology subprocessors (specifically Huntress Labs Inc. and Microsoft Corporation) who:
* Need to know such information to execute the Authorized Purpose; and
* Are bound by written confidentiality agreements or professional ethical duties at least as restrictive as those contained herein.

4.4 **Compelled Disclosures:** If the Receiving Party is legally compelled by court order, subpoena, or government regulatory body to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall (to the extent legally permissible) provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice within forty-eight (48) hours so the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or appropriate remedy.

---

### 5. Term & Post-Termination Survival

5.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall remain in effect for three (3) years from the Effective Date, unless superseded by an executed Master Services Agreement.

5.2 **Survival of Obligations:** The confidentiality and non-use obligations with respect to all Confidential Information disclosed during the term shall survive for a period of **three (3) years** following the termination or expiration of this Agreement.  
* *Trade Secrets Exception:* Any Confidential Information qualifying as a Trade Secret under the Texas Uniform Trade Secrets Act (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 134A) shall be protected indefinitely for so long as such information remains a trade secret under Texas law.

---

### 6. Return or Secure Destruction of Materials

Upon written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly:
* Cease all use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;
* Securely destroy or return all documents, materials, and electronic files containing Confidential Information; and
* Provide written certification within fourteen (14) days confirming cryptographic deletion or physical destruction;  
*(provided, however, that the Receiving Party may retain one (1) secure archival copy solely for legal, regulatory, or compliance audit purposes, subject to ongoing confidentiality obligations).*

---

### 7. Equitable Relief & Enforcement

The Parties acknowledge that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, in the event of a breach or threatened breach, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable remedies in a court of competent jurisdiction without the necessity of posting a bond or proving actual monetary damages, in addition to any other remedies available under Texas law.

---

### 8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

8.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Texas, without giving effect to any choice of law rules.

8.2 **Venue:** Any legal action, suit, or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Harris County, Houston, Texas, and each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive personal jurisdiction and venue of such courts.
