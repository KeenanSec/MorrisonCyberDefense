### Contract Flow Architecture for New Engagements

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Lead["1. Prospect / Insurance Referral"] --> NDA["2. Mutual NDA (Optional Pre-Discovery)"]
    NDA --> SOW1["3. SOW: Tier 1 Diagnostic Audit ($2,500)"]
    SOW1 --> MSA["4. Master Services Agreement (MSA) + RoE Authorization"]
    MSA --> Deposit["5. 50% Upfront Deposit ($1,250)"]
    Deposit --> Audit["6. Non-Disruptive Audit & Executive Risk Scorecard"]
    Audit --> RollIn["7. 100% Roll-In Credit Applied to Sprint or Retainer"]
    RollIn --> Sprint["8. Tier 2 Hardening Sprint ($3,800) OR Tier 3 Retainer ($1,750/mo)"]
```
