# Evidence-based assessment

Trigger: An authorized diagnostic engagement.

Ownership: Keenan collects and drafts; Principal reviews interpretation and final findings.

1. Read scope and authorization before collection. Confirm tenant and assets. Use documented read-only collection methods; logging or a dry-run flag alone is not proof that a command cannot modify state.

2. Record collection time, collector, source/tool version, approved storage reference, and limitations in evidence.csv. Keep raw sensitive evidence in the agreed restricted storage.

3. Create findings.csv entries linking evidence IDs to observed condition, business impact, severity rationale, recommendation, owner, and validation approach. Mark unknowns explicitly.

4. Draft report.md using only verified observations. Existing assessment templates contain examples: do not copy their findings, scores, percentages, or completed-review language as facts.

5. Principal reviews scope coverage, evidence, risk rationale, and the prioritized roadmap. Record unresolved limitations and report approval before delivery.

Completion: Reviewed report, evidence index, and actionable findings backlog; delivery and receipt recorded.
