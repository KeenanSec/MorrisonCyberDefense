# Prospecting and referrals

Trigger: A lead list, inbound inquiry, or partner introduction.

Ownership: Keenan prepares research and drafts; Principal reviews service fit and commitments.

1. Copy selected CSV leads into the workspace pipeline.csv register. Registry entries are prospects, not verified buyers. Deduplicate and verify organization, contact details, source date, and fit.

2. Record qualification: business need, decision maker, timing, existing IT provider, proposed next action, owner, and due date in the pipeline.

3. Use sales-and-gtm scripts as drafting references. Confirm current pricing and avoid unsupported security or conversion claims.

4. Prepare a personalized draft. Sending outreach requires an explicit instruction; the harness never sends messages. Record opt-outs as do-not-contact and check them before every outreach.

5. Log response, follow-up, referral source, and disposition. Move qualified opportunities to discovery; record why others were closed.

Completion: A qualified opportunity with a named owner and next action, or a documented closed/do-not-contact disposition.
