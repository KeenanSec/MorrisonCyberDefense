# Quality review and data handling

Trigger: Before collection, sharing, or client delivery.

Ownership: Author prepares evidence; Principal reviews client-facing conclusions.

1. Choose approved storage and access restrictions for each client. Keep client work out of version control; ignored files are still readable by local users and backups.

2. Use evidence references instead of copying credentials, personal information, or raw tenant exports into reports or prompts. Confirm permission before putting client data into an external service.

3. Review report accuracy, evidence links, scope coverage, placeholders, severity rationale, limitations, and client separation. Distinguish templates and forecasts from actual results.

4. Record reviewer, date, exact artifact/version, and decision in approvals.md. Send only the approved artifact through the agreed channel after an explicit sending instruction.

5. Apply the agreed retention schedule, access reviews, and backup/restore checks. Record disposal and exceptions; do not invent a universal retention period.

Completion: Traceable review and storage decisions, with no unsupported completion claims.
