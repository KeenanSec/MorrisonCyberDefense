# Engagement closeout and offboarding

Trigger: Completion, termination, or transition to another provider.

Ownership: Principal confirms acceptance and obligations; Keenan executes the documented handoff.

1. Confirm delivered artifacts, unresolved findings, accepted exceptions, and the receiving owner. Record acceptance or outstanding disputes.

2. Hand over the approved documentation, configuration ownership, and open work through agreed channels. Confirm receipt.

3. Revoke engagement access and service accounts only according to the agreed transition plan. Verify that monitoring and operational ownership have transferred.

4. Reconcile final invoice references, licenses, subscriptions, and renewal responsibilities. Avoid cancelling a client dependency without authorization.

5. Record retention/disposal dates, access removal evidence, lessons learned, and remaining commitments in closeout.md.

Completion: Client handoff, access disposition, financial follow-up, and retention actions are recorded.
