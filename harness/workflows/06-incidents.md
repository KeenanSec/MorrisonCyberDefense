# Incident intake and coordination

Trigger: A client report or alert suggesting an incident.

Ownership: Principal coordinates; Keenan records evidence and carries out authorized tasks.

1. Open incident.md and record detection time/timezone, reporter, affected client/assets, symptoms, source, and known impact. Distinguish observed facts from hypotheses.

2. Contact the designated Principal and contracted response provider through the agreed channel. Confirm the responder and client decision maker; do not assume this folder provides 24/7 response.

3. Preserve relevant evidence in approved storage and keep a timestamped action log. Record who authorized each containment or recovery action.

4. Coordinate changes with the authorized responder. Route notification decisions to the client and their designated advisers; avoid speculative breach statements.

5. Document recovery validation, client status updates, handoff, and a retrospective with corrective tasks.

Completion: Named responder accepts the handoff or recovery is documented; follow-up actions are assigned.
