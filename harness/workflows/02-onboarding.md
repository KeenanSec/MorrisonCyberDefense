# Discovery and client onboarding

Trigger: A qualified opportunity moving toward an engagement.

Ownership: Principal owns scope and client authorization; Keenan prepares the workspace.

1. Create an engagement with the CLI. Record business objectives, stakeholders, exact tenant/assets, exclusions, dependencies, delivery dates, and success criteria in scope.md.

2. Prepare a proposal using the existing pricing references after confirming the offer. Track commercial agreement, billing milestone, and authorization references; do not store payment details here.

3. Use the existing rules-of-engagement template as a drafting reference. Record actual signed authorization location and permitted activities in approvals.md.

4. Confirm emergency contacts, client decision maker, access method, evidence storage, retention agreement, and subcontractor responsibilities.

5. Grant only the access needed through the agreed credential system. Store vault references, never passwords or tokens. Schedule kickoff and assign tasks.

Completion: Scope and authorization are recorded, contacts and access are confirmed, and work has an owner and date.
