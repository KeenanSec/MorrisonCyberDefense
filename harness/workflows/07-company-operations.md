# Weekly company execution

Trigger: The weekly founders meeting.

Ownership: Keenan and Principal share planning; each action has one accountable owner.

1. Review the dashboard, overdue tasks, opportunity follow-ups, active engagements, and upcoming client commitments.

2. Choose the week’s priorities based on capacity. Record owner, due date, expected result, and blockers for each task.

3. Review the business register for formation, insurance, accounting, agreements, invoicing, collections, domain ownership, backups, vendor access, and renewals. Record actual status and evidence references; a README checklist is not proof of completion.

4. Track booked work, collected revenue, delivery hours, direct costs, and pipeline outcomes in metrics.csv. Define each measure and reporting period.

5. Write material decisions and assumptions in decisions.md. Confirm handoffs and update next-review dates before ending the meeting.

Completion: Updated tasks, decisions, business obligations, and an achievable weekly plan.
