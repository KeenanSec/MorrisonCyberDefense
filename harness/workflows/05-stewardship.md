# Monthly client stewardship

Trigger: A contracted recurring service review.

Ownership: Keenan prepares the review; Principal prioritizes and reviews client recommendations.

1. Confirm contracted coverage, client inventory, support hours, escalation contacts, renewal dates, and provider responsibilities. Do not infer active monitoring from a plan to buy a tool.

2. Review approved monitoring exports, configuration drift, outstanding findings, access changes, backup test evidence, and service tickets against the agreed scope.

3. Update monthly-review.md with reporting period, evidence, changes, incidents, exceptions, overdue actions, and proposed priorities.

4. Create owned tasks for gaps and follow the change workflow for remediation. Schedule client review and record decisions.

5. Reconcile service hours, licensing costs, invoice references, and renewal actions. Track actual metrics rather than projected results.

Completion: Reviewed monthly report and agreed next actions with owners and dates.
